How an engagement runs

Four steps, one lens.

Nothing is installed before it is understood. The lens stays the same throughout — intent, system, friction, outcome, learning.

Diagnose

We sit with the work as it really happens and write down what we see — intent first, friction second.

Design

We draft the smallest system that would close the gap, on paper, before anything is built.

Install

We build it on your machine, in plain text, and walk it into your week until it holds.

Measure

We come back to the outcome, honestly, and adjust what the numbers and the people tell us to.

Every step is read through the five-step lens:  Intent → System → Friction → Outcome → Learning
