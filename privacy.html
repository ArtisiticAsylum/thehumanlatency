Privacy

Privacy Policy.

Last updated 28 August 2026. This policy covers the humanlatency.online website and the private publishing tool ("yt-uploader") that The Human Latency uses to upload its own videos to its own YouTube channels.

The website

What this site collects: almost nothing.

This is a static website. It has no accounts, no comments, no analytics scripts, and no advertising. If you join the letter, your email address is used only to send it and is never sold or shared. Fonts are served by Google Fonts, whose handling of requests is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

YouTube API Services

Our publishing tool and YouTube.

The Human Latency uses a private, internal tool that uses YouTube API Services to upload finished videos to YouTube channels owned by The Human Latency. By using this tool we agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

  • The tool is used only by the channel owner, on the owner's own computer, for the owner's own channels. It has no other users.
  • It accesses only the ability to upload videos (the YouTube "upload" permission). It does not read, collect, store, or share any other person's data, and it does not access viewer or subscriber information.
  • The only data stored are Google's own sign-in tokens for the owner's accounts, kept in local files on the owner's computer. They are never transmitted anywhere except to Google itself, and are never shared with any third party.
  • No advertising or analytics services receive anything from this tool.
Deletion and revocation

Removing stored data.

The stored sign-in tokens can be deleted at any time by deleting the token files from the owner's computer. Access granted to the tool can also be revoked at any time from Google's security settings at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Once revoked, the tool can no longer act on the account. No other user data exists to delete.
